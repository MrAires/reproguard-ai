REPROGUARD AI - WINDOWS QUICK START
===================================

1. Keep this folder in a permanent location, for example:
   C:\ReproGuardAI

2. Make sure Python 3.10 or newer is installed.
   During Python installation, enable "Add Python to PATH".

3. Double-click:
   setup_windows.bat

4. Wait until you see "Setup complete".
   A "ReproGuard AI" shortcut will be created on your Desktop.

5. Double-click the Desktop shortcut.
   ReproGuard will open in your browser at:
   http://127.0.0.1:8501

6. In the GUI:
   - click "Run bundled demo paper" first, or
   - upload your own research-paper PDF.

7. To stop the local server, double-click:
   stop_reproguard.bat

IMPORTANT
---------
- The RCI is a reproducibility-documentation completeness measure.
- It is NOT a score of scientific truth, novelty, or research quality.
- When Online verification is enabled, detected DOI and GitHub identifiers are queried against public APIs.
- Launcher logs are stored in logs\reproguard_launcher.log.
